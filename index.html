    Steer your ship, dodge the meteor shower, and survive as long as you can.

    Selected Pilot: Nova
    How to play
    • Move: Arrow keys or WASD. Move your mouse over the game to steer.
    • Shoot: Press F, J, or K, or click the game.
    • Survive: Dodge meteors, drones, satellites, and enemy shots. You have 3 lives.
    • Power-ups: Collect glowing drops for rapid fire, spread shots, shields, or an extra life.
    • Bosses: Boss waves arrive as your score grows. Keep moving and aim for the boss.
    • Pause: Press P or use the Pause button.
